## FAQ

**1. Is this really free?**
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no signup required. You see your audit results before we ask for anything. We're built by Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — so when you save money, we earn the chance to help you save even more.

**2. How accurate are the savings estimates?**
Every number in our audit engine is based on official vendor pricing pages, verified the week of launch. We don't inflate savings or make up numbers. If your stack is already optimized, we'll tell you that honestly.

**3. What AI tools do you support?**
Currently: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Google Gemini, and v0 by Vercel. We're adding more every month.

**4. What happens after I enter my email?**
You get a confirmation email with a link to your full report. If your savings are over $500/month, a Credex consultant may reach out to help you capture those savings through discounted credits. That's it — no spam, no sales funnel.

**5. Can I share my audit results?**
Yes! Every audit gets a unique public URL you can share with your team or on social media. Personal details (email, company name) are stripped from the public version — only tools and savings numbers are visible.
